Our Mission & Values

At PastelPages, we aim to create a peaceful online space where book lovers can explore a vast collection of titles in a calming environment. We are committed to promoting literacy, accessibility, and user satisfaction, ensuring everyone can enjoy the pleasure of reading.

User-focused design

Built with simplicity and comfort in mind, our interface helps readers focus on what truly matters: the joy of discovering great books.

Accessible 24/7

Our library is always open — explore our digital shelves anytime, from anywhere, and discover something new whenever inspiration strikes.

Growing collection

We’re continuously adding new titles to our catalog to support students and readers with diverse interests, from fiction to technical resources.

Student project

This digital library was created as part of a student initiative to combine web development with a love for books — a modern solution for managing reading resources.
